       9.  CustomerServices

       This chapter describes MPT support services that are
       available for the MPT 1.9 release.

       9.1  SupportPolicy

       SGI offers standard support for problems in the following
       categories:

          +o Build problems

          +o Run-time problems specific to SGI computer systems

       Fixes to problems will be made available through one of the
       following mechanisms:

          +o Upgrade utility

          +o Major release

          +o Minor release

       9.1.1  MPISupportPolicy
       SGI will address MPI problems in a timely fashion.

       9.1.2  SHMEMLibrarySupportPolicy
       SGI will address SHMEM message passing problems in a timely
       fashion.

       9.4  SoftwareProblemReportingandResolutionProcess

       If you experience problems with MPT 1.9, contact your
       service representative. Your service representative will
       work with you to resolve the problem. If you choose to have
       full-time or part-time on-site support, your on-site support
       personnel are your primary contacts for service. If you have
       elected not to have on-site support, please call the
       Customer Support Center and report your problem to them.

       When you report problems, use the product name most
       appropriate for the problem and refer to MPT for all
       products. Use any of the following product names:

          +o MPI

          +o LIBSMA

       9.5  Pipeline

       Customers who have a support contract receive Pipeline, the
       SGI customer newsletter. Pipeline provides product and
       support information about SGI workstations and servers.
       Pipeline is published six times a year (January/February,
       March/April, and so on). Pipeline is available on the World
       Wide Web in Supportfolio Online (http://support.sgi.com/).

       9.6  AdditionalSupportResources

       Along with the standard support described in the preceding
       sections, users of MPI have a variety of other resources
       available to help them use these products. These resources
       should supplement, not replace, standard SGI support. In
       particular, users are encouraged to use SGI support when
       they encounter what may be an MPI bug, even if the problem
       does not apply to SGI systems. However, these other
       resources can be helpful in gaining a better understanding
       of MPI and how it can be used. The following sections
       describe some of these resources.

       9.6.1  MPIStandard
       You can access the MPI standard in any of the following

       9.6.2  NewsGroups
       The comp.parallel.mpi Internet news groups focus on MPI and
       related products. The MPI developers use these news groups
       to announce new releases of the products and to discuss
       related topics.  Users are also free to use the news groups
       to interact with one another by responding to postings made
       by others.

       To access comp.parallel.mpi, you must have access to the
       Internet and a news reader program. If necessary, contact
       your site administrator for additional information.

       9.6.3  MPIUserGroupMeetings
       You can obtain information about upcoming MPI developers
       conferences and user group meetings through
       comp.parallel.mpi or through the MPI web page listed in "MPI
       Standard".

       The MPI Forum e-mail discussions, and both current and
       earlier versions of the standard, are available from the
       netlib server. For more information about netlib, see the
       following section.

       9.6.4  netlibSource

       called netlib. If your site is using the versions of these
       products contained in MPT, you will not need to obtain the

       resources available from netlib might be useful:

          +o Test and sample programs contained in the public domain
            releases that are not included in the SGI release

          +o Papers and presentations by the MPI developers that
            describe MPI and related products
